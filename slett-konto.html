Delete account and data

Information on how to request deletion of your Stockd account.

1. How to request deletion

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, send an email to sandertennstrand@gmail.com.

Please include the email address associated with your account so we can process your request faster.

2. What gets deleted

Upon approval of your request, we delete your account and data associated with it, including:

  • Login information stored via Firebase Authentication.
  • Inventory, shopping list, and history associated with your account.
  • Any household data that is linked solely to your user, where applicable.

3. Processing time

We process deletion requests as quickly as possible and no later than within 30 days.

4. Back to the privacy policy

You can return to the privacy policy for more information about how data is processed in Stockd.
